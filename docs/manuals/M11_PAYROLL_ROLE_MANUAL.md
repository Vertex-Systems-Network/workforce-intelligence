# WorkIntel Payroll Manager Role Manual

Payroll starts with evidence: resolve Attendance and approved Time before calculating or finalizing Payroll. Then review Compliance, Finance adjustments and Reports. Approved payroll is historical evidence; do not alter calculation rules merely to force a desired result when source attendance/time inputs are wrong.

The Start Here checklist is personal and does not alter payroll state. Use it as an operating routine, while workflow status, approvals and locked payroll snapshots remain the system of record.
