# WorkIntel HR Role Manual

The HR path begins with People, HRIS and Organization structure, then moves to Attendance, Leave and Performance. Accurate people/lifecycle records are the source context for downstream policy, reporting and approval behavior. Review organization relationships before changing manager-sensitive workflows or scopes.

Use Help Center contextual guidance on each HR page. It explains the local workflow without bypassing effective permissions or exposing people outside the member's current data scope.
